# Print Room HQ

The print room dashboard for Bambu Lab, Snapmaker U1 and Klipper printers: every printer on one screen with live cameras, a print queue, AI failure watching, spools and costs, quotes and your model library.

## Getting started

1. Start the add-on. The first start downloads Print Room HQ from printroomhq.com (a minute or two), then it updates itself from there.
2. Click **Open Web UI**. The first-run setup finds the printers on your network.
3. To see your printers as Home Assistant devices too, open Print Room HQ's **Settings › Home Assistant** and point it at your MQTT broker (the Mosquitto add-on works).

Your printers, settings and history live in the add-on's storage and are part of your Home Assistant backups.

The full guide: https://printroomhq.com/guide/docker/#home-assistant
